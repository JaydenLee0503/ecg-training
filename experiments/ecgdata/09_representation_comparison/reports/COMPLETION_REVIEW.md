# ECG-09 completion review — 2026-11-07 EDT

The prespecified existing-cohort representation comparison finished and its independent saved-artifact audit passed. The full numerical report, per-seed class metrics, confusion matrices, paired bootstrap results, per-fit runtimes and recorded warnings are in [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) and [analysis.json](analysis.json); the checks are in [audit.json](audit.json). The study used the existing five patient-separated ECG-07 folds, with no additional patients or exclusions.

On the same 1,620 original evaluation windows from 80 patients, mean window macro-F1 was 0.8846 for log-band Swin and 0.8390 for LFCC Swin. The paired difference was +0.0457 (95% patient-cluster bootstrap interval +0.0202 to +0.0800). Patient-vote accuracy was 98.33% versus 95.83%; its paired interval for the difference was 0.0 to +5.42 percentage points, so the patient-vote difference is uncertain. The matched log-band logistic control achieved 0.8345 window macro-F1. The fixed raw CNN achieved 0.5795, a negative result for that particular model and protocol.

The primary evaluation uses the original v1 subset; all-window results cover all 21,222 windows, not new independent patients. The paired intervals use 2,000 patient-cluster draws, bootstrap seed 20260925, and are pointwise conditional on the saved fits; they do not include retraining or selection uncertainty. This small cohort has been examined repeatedly, and diagnosis is confounded with data source. There is no external or clinical validation. The log-band comparison changes input width and projection parameters as well as representation; the raw comparison changes architecture and capacity. These results cannot identify an isolated causal effect or establish that other CNNs perform similarly.

The five folds and initialization seeds 0, 1 and 2 produced 15 log-band Swin and 15 raw CNN fits; five deterministic log-band logistic fits bring the new total to 35. The LFCC controls were reused from ECG-07. Sum of new fit times was 6,343.7 seconds, excluding feature preparation and reporting; this is not elapsed wall-clock time. At restart, 23 completed fits and the interrupted epoch-6 checkpoint were integrity-checked and reused. The independent audit checked all 35 fit markers, 35 model reload spot checks, 15 Swin normalizers, patient isolation, 10 aggregate metric sets and the primary bootstrap; numerical checks cannot prove the absence of software defects. Feature reconstruction maximum absolute LFCC difference was 7.63e-6. Original driver version was not recorded; the restart driver was 610.88. Other recorded environment and GPU model matched.
