# ACS/OMI patient-grouped undersampling, v1

Prospective comparison: train LFCC logistic (C=1) and the original zero-dropout LFCC Swin (40 epochs, seeds 0/1/2) on an equal number of positive and negative ECGs by reducing negative training patients. Retain all positive subfit patients and all their ECGs, including any negative ECGs from mixed-label patients. Select negative-only patient groups by a seeded deterministic subset-sum to reach the exact positive ECG count without splitting a patient's records. Seed 20261031. No oversampling or class weighting. Normalize using only selected unique training ECGs.

Reuse the sensitivity study's subfit/calibration/validation partitions: 11,458/2,866/3,581 ECGs, patient disjoint. The calibration and validation cohorts retain natural prevalence. Before validation scoring, freeze the highest calibration thresholds satisfying 80/90/95% sensitivity, retaining ties. Compare with the same-subfit oversampled LFCC logistic and Swin from the completed sensitivity study, which has identical calibration and validation patients. The prespecified primary is validation AP of undersampled logistic minus oversampled logistic; Swin and threshold metrics are secondary. Use 2,000 paired validation-patient bootstrap draws, seed 20261031. Report all initializations, per-record predictions, calibration thresholds, patient selection, fits, time and warnings. Intervals condition on fitted models and sampled patients; comparisons also change training sample size and normalization. The validation cohort has been repeatedly examined. The official test waveforms remain reserved.
